# Marketplaces and revenue model

## Rentals marketplace

- Owners list aircraft with hourly rates, requirements, and availability.
- Renters request bookings with date/hour selection and pay via PayPal.
- Messaging opens only during active rentals to reduce abuse.
- Reviews are enabled post-rental.

## Services marketplace

Categories supported:

- Aircraft sales
- Charter services
- Flight instructors (CFI)
- Flight schools
- Aircraft mechanics
- Aviation jobs

## Pricing and monetization

- Rentals: platform fee percentage split between renter and owner.
- Marketplace: tiered paid listings with durations and upgrades.

## Payments and payouts

- PayPal Orders API for checkout.
- Payouts to owners require Braintree Marketplace approval (blocker).
