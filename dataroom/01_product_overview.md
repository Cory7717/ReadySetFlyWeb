# Product overview

## Summary

ReadySetFly is a dual marketplace for general aviation:

- Rentals marketplace (Airbnb-style) for aircraft hourly bookings.
- Services marketplace (classifieds-style) for sales, CFIs, schools, mechanics, charter, and jobs.

## Core value proposition

- Single account for pilots, owners, and service providers.
- Trust layer with identity and document verification.
- Messaging gate tied to rental status to reduce fraud and privacy risk.

## Trust and safety layers

- Email and phone verification.
- Government ID (front/back) and selfie liveness check.
- Optional FAA certificate verification.
- Payment method on file requirement.
- Admin suspension for expired documents.

## Business model

- Rentals: platform fee percentage per booking.
- Marketplace listings: tiered paid listings (duration-based).

## Current status (from internal analysis)

- Web app, backend, and database are production-ready.
- Key blockers remain for owner payouts, messaging E2E validation, and automated rental completion.
